# What fits in next year's phone

Estimates for a phone shipping in 2007, at a million units. Parts cost is per phone.

| Idea                                  | Parts cost                | Battery | Months | Fits?                     |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------- | ------ | ------------------------- |
| Real web browser, pages zoomed to fit | $18 (3.5-inch screen)     | Medium  | 9      | **Yes**                   |
| Music player built in                 | $6 (more storage)         | Low     | 5      | **Yes**                   |
| Send a photo to the web               | $2                        | Low     | 4      | **Yes**                   |
| On-screen keyboard                    | $0 (needs the big screen) | Low     | 8      | With the browser          |
| Push email for everyone               | $0                        | Medium  | 6      | Needs a server deal       |
| Programs from other companies         | $0                        | Varies  | 12+    | Not next year             |
| GPS and maps                          | $11                       | High    | 10     | No: battery               |
| Two-day battery                       | $9                        | n/a     | 6      | No: fights the big screen |

## Notes

- The browser and the on-screen keyboard share one part: the 3.5-inch touch screen. Buy one, get both.
- GPS drains a full battery in about four hours of use. Revisit when the chips get better.
- The RAZR shows people pay more for a thin phone. A bigger screen has to stay thin.
