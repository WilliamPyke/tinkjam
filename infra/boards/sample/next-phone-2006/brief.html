Concept for 2007

The pocket web

The whole web, not WAP pages, on a 3.5-inch screen. Open any page, see all of it, and zoom in with a thumb.

Why this one

Strongest theme"It's not the real internet." Elena's research, theme 1
Fits the phone$18 in parts, 9 months. Marcus's table
Helps photos tooThe same screen shows photos worth sending (theme 3)
One part, two ideasThe touch screen also gives us an on-screen keyboard

What we'd build

  • A full browserReal pages, shown whole, then zoomed to the column you tap.
  • A 3.5-inch touch screenThin like the RAZR, no flip.
  • An on-screen keyboardShows only when you type, so the page gets the whole screen.

What we'd leave out

  • GPS and maps: too hard on the battery this year
  • Programs from other companies: revisit for 2008

How we'd test it

Put a mock-up in the hands of 12 commuters. Can they read a news site and write a short email on the screen keyboard, on a moving train, in under two minutes?
