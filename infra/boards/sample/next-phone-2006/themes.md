# Research themes, March 2006

From 10 interviews and two days riding commuter trains. Four themes, strongest first.

## 1. The phone web isn't the real web

People have tried the web on their phones and given up. WAP pages feel like a different, smaller internet.

> "It's not the real internet. I wait until I'm at my desk."

## 2. Too many gadgets in one pocket

Six of the ten carry a phone and an iPod. Two carry a camera too. Nobody likes it; they put up with it.

> "Phone, iPod, keys. I check for all three every time I stand up."

## 3. Photos are stuck on the phone

Everyone takes camera-phone photos. Almost nobody sends them anywhere.

> "How would I even send them? I'd need a cable."

## 4. Thumb typing matters more than we thought

Commuters write real email on the train, with both thumbs. Keyboard phones win them over; T9 is put up with.

> "BlackBerry is the only phone I can actually write on."

## What this means

A bigger, better screen helps themes 1 and 3 at once. Theme 4 is the risk: if a bigger screen costs us the keyboard, the thumb typists need a good answer.
